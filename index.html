Questions, answered.

What is an AI DM Setter?

An assistant that replies to inbound DMs, answers common questions, qualifies leads and guides them toward booking.

Does it replace my sales team?

No. It handles first response and qualification so your team spends its time on serious prospects.

What platforms can it work with?

It depends on your setup. We confirm the supported channels for your business during your demo.

Can it book appointments?

Yes, it is built to move qualified prospects to a booking, connected to your calendar during setup.

Can I control what the AI says?

Yes. Your offers, answers, tone and rules are defined with you before launch.

What happens when a lead wants a human?

Handoff rules route the conversation to your team with the full history.

How long does setup take?

It depends on scope. We share a timeline after learning how you sell.

Do I need a CRM?

No. We can work with your existing one or build a Sales Command Center for you.

Can you build the website too?

Yes, through the Complete Business Upgrade.

Can the system handle calls?

Yes, with the AI Receptionist.

Can I cancel?

Terms are set out in your agreement, and we walk through them with you before you commit.
